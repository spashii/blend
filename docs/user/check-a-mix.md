# Check a mix

Click the headphones button next to a mix in the menu bar panel to hear what apps reading it get.

- **On headphones**, you hear the mix live until you click it again.
- **On speakers**, Blend Mic records 5 seconds with a countdown, then plays them back. Listening live through speakers would feed back into the microphone.

Bluetooth headphones and the headphone jack count as headphones. USB, HDMI and AirPlay outputs count as speakers, because they may be.

A check runs the mix's sources even when no app is using it. The recording stays in memory and is discarded after it plays. Closing every Blend Mic window and the panel ends a check.
