# Play music into an online call

A mix of your microphone and a music app, picked as the microphone in Discord or another call app, sends both to the call. Each source has its own volume, so the music can sit under your voice.

## Set it up

1. Click **+** in the menu bar panel. Pick your microphone and create the mix.
2. In **Settings → (your mix) → Sources**, click **Add Source…** and add the music app, or a browser playing it.
3. In the call app, pick the mix as your microphone.
4. Set the volumes in the menu bar panel by expanding the mix.

To send everything your Mac plays instead of one app, turn on **System audio** when you create the mix.

## Turn Split by channel off

A mix of a microphone and System audio starts split: your voice on the left, the rest on the right. Some call apps read only one channel, so the other side would hear only your voice. Turn off **Split by channel** for a mix a call app reads.

## A call through a mix never hears itself

System audio leaves out every app that reads the mix, so the call does not echo back to the people in it. If you add the call app itself as a source, Blend Mic warns that it hears itself.

## If the music sounds choppy on the other end

Call apps run their own noise suppression, which can treat music as noise. Turn it down in the call app: in Zoom, turn on "Original sound for musicians"; in Discord, turn off noise suppression.

## Browsers

A browser is added whole, every tab in that copy. Browser profiles can be added on their own only when each runs as its own copy, as Firefox profiles do. Profiles opened from Chrome's profile menu share one copy and cannot be split.
